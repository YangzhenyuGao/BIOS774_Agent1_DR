Interpretation and limitations

On this final cohort, tsne has the highest observed trustworthiness (0.897), while tsne has the highest neighbor recall (0.208). The first two PCA components explain 3.8% of variance in the preprocessed feature space. These diagnostics describe different aspects of the embedding and need not identify the same method as best.

The shortlist reflects measured preservation of local geometry in the declared input representation under the specified compute budget. It does not establish a universally superior method. Changes in preprocessing, cohort composition, neighborhood scale, or objective weights can change the ranking. Linear PCA remains an interpretable baseline. Disconnected graphs and numerical warnings are retained in the evidence and affect eligibility.

GPLVM uses actual variational optimization with latent uncertainty and inducing GP variables. Its fixed budget does not prove convergence; loss history and latent displacement are saved. Successful-run runtime includes method imports, input loading and fitting, but excludes subprocess startup and common imports. Cold caches and hardware can affect timing. A conservative cubic bound informs PathMNIST cohort planning but is not a fitted complexity model. Biological and histological labels are diagnostics, not validation of causal or biological mechanisms. PBMC annotations derive from a prior processed clustering and are not independent ground truth.
